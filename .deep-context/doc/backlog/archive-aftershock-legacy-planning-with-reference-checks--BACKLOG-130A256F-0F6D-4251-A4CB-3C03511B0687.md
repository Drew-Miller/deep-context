# Archive Aftershock legacy planning with reference checks

Classify older Aftershock plans, packets, reports, and nested planning files; promote unique guidance, preserve evidence, rewrite inbound references, then archive truly historical material outside ordinary context routes.

## Starting inventory

Aftershock has 126 Markdown files in the outer `docs/plan/` tree. At least 56 have direct task-state or validator dependencies, and other files may have transitive links or unique evidence. The six files under the nested `Aftershock/docs/plan/` tree have no current task or canonical-route references, but their old state and reports preserve historical facts and all six have source-inventory records.

## Proposed work

- Classify each legacy document as current authority, forwarding entry, historical evidence, or review required. Record incoming task, packet, link, validator, and inventory references before moving anything.
- Start with the nested historical plan tree and stale outer overview material. Promote unique current guidance into canonical Deep Context documents, preserve original evidence, and keep minimal redirects where references cannot yet move.
- Move eligible history out of ordinary context routes, update provenance and required reads, then refresh indexes and validate. Deletion requires proof that no unique evidence or required reference is lost.

## Done when

- The normal route contains no competing current guidance; all live task paths, historical evidence links, context validation, and Aftershock source-ownership restrictions still work.
