# Knowledge and discussion history

Current requirements describe accepted behavior; decisions record accepted choices and rationale. Design interviews and follow-up notes preserve the discussion that led to them. Existing interviews and notes may contain earlier alternatives or unresolved questions; they do not override current requirements. New dropped or replaced work requests do not need permanent history.

Keep actionable follow-up requests and unresolved questions in transient work records. Save substantive discussion history when requested or when implemented, accepted behavior needs its rationale preserved. Do not automatically archive every rejected or replaced work request. Use descriptive dated files in `docs/notes/`, and link them from the relevant work item or plan. Preserve the user's meaning and distinguish quotations from summaries. Routine conversation does not require a note.

When accepted requirements change, update their canonical home, preserve meaningful earlier reasoning in linked history, and identify superseded guidance and its replacement. Do not duplicate full requirements into notes or claim automatic version snapshots. Plans and tickets link to current requirements; history is consulted only when relevant.

## Current groups and retired guidance

Group the Knowledge index by reader purpose. Setup and structure, planning and work, context and browsing, and decisions and rationale are this project's starting groups. Agents may adapt the grouping to a project's actual usage while keeping each accepted rule in its canonical document.

When a canonical entry or accepted guidance is withdrawn or superseded, remove its active index entry and record a short dated summary under a separate Retired guidance heading. Explain what it covered, why it changed and what remains authoritative. Keep retirement summaries outside the active tables. Remove the former hyperlink rather than deliberately leaving a broken link to a document that might be deleted. Retiring an index label does not retire its source document or all the guidance it contains.

Retirement summaries do not require permanent history for every abandoned or replaced work request. Source-only Notes and Interviews may preserve design reasoning but are not published in the dashboard.
